Tips & Tricks

How do I know how much storage space I will need?
Our units range in size from 10 x 10 mini storage units, each fitting the contents of a small bedroom, to 10 x 15, which is big enough to fit the contents of and entire family room and two full bedrooms.

Are there any items that I am NOT able to store?
Liquids, explosives, flammable liquids, toxic materials and perishables.
